Quick answers

How do I make a Platform Pack? On the first screen pick Social, Work or Dating and tap Choose a photo (or use the sample portrait). Then tap Save my Platform Pack. Every size goes straight into your Photos app, each framed on your face, and the saved set appears on screen.

Where do saved photos go? Straight into your Photos app. The first time, iPhone asks for permission to add photos.

No face found? Use a photo where the face is reasonably large, front-lit and not covered. In group photos, tap the person you want to edit.

The makeup looks too strong or too light. Use How strong under Looks, or fine-tune each tool in the Beauty tab.

Do my photos leave my phone? No. Everything happens on your iPhone and the app sends nothing to us.

Delete my data Settings → Delete my data clears saved looks and drafts on your iPhone. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
