Face Couture for iPhone

The iPhone app stores nothing about you outside your iPhone, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

To clear saved looks and drafts on your iPhone: open Settings (the gear) → Delete my data. Deleting the app also removes everything.

The web version

Your photos, drafts and saved looks stay in your browser. Delete them under My looks & drafts, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

If you used the web version's optional AI tools, our server keeps a random ID with AI-allowance counts (no photos). To delete it, email hello@facecouture.app and we will delete it within 7 days and confirm. Photos sent for an AI edit are not stored by us.
