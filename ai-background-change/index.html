AI background change: put yourself anywhere

Turn an ordinary photo into a destination shot: a city skyline, a beach at sunset, a rooftop or a glam restaurant.

Two background tools

  • Background swap (free): finds you in the photo on your phone and puts a clean color, blur or your own image behind you. Nothing is uploaded.
  • AI Background Change: places you in world cities, landmarks, beaches and glam venues with matching light.

Tips for the best result

  • Pick a destination with light similar to your photo.
