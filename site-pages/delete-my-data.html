Delete my data

Face Couture has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is what exists and how to remove it.

On your device

Your photos, drafts and saved looks live only on your device. Delete them under My looks & drafts, or delete the app (iPhone/Android) or clear site data (browser).

On our server

A random install ID with AI-allowance counts and promo-code use. To delete it: open the app → Settings (gear icon) → Delete my data. It is removed immediately.

No app access? Email hello@facecouture.app with the Device ID shown at the bottom of the app's Settings screen, and we will delete it within 7 days and confirm.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are held by Apple or Google. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play account; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

AI provider

Photos sent for an AI Look are not stored by us. OpenAI's API retains inputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and then deletes them.
