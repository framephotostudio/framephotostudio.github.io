Quick answers

Where do saved photos go? Tap Save, then "Save Image" on the sheet: the photo lands in your Photos app (Camera Roll on Android). Videos save the same way.
No face found? Use a photo where the face is reasonably large, front-lit and not covered. Group photos: tap the person you want to edit. Tiny faces in the background are ignored on purpose.
The makeup looks too strong or too light. Use the strength row (Subtle · Natural · Glam · Max). Glam and Max keep your own choices and intensify them.
Do my photos leave my phone? No, unless you choose an AI Look and tick the consent box. See the Privacy Policy.
AI says I have used my looks for today. Allowances reset daily and monthly. Failed edits never count. Pro raises the allowance when available.
Subscription or refund — manage it in your App Store or Google Play account. Refunds are issued by Apple or Google.
Promo code — Settings (gear icon) → Enter a promo code.
Report an AI result — on the AI screen tap "Report this AI result". We review every report.
Delete my data — Settings → Delete my data, or use this page.
