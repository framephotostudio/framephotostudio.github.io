Face Couture by Always Luxe · website and iPhone/Android apps · Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version. Your photos stay on your device. Face and lip mapping for makeup runs on your phone and is never uploaded, stored or shared. There are no accounts. The only time a photo leaves your device is when you choose an optional AI Look and tick the consent box; it is then sent securely to our AI provider for that single edit, returned to you, and not kept by us.

2. Face data

Face Couture detects facial landmarks (eyes, lips, brows, face outline) to position makeup. This happens entirely on your device using open-source models that are bundled with the app (website visitors download them from Google Cloud Storage and jsDelivr the first time). Landmarks are not biometric templates, are not stored, are not used to identify anyone and never leave your device. We do not sell or share face data, and we do not use it to train AI.

3. AI Looks and consent

AI Looks are optional and off until you choose one. Before the first AI edit you must tick a consent box confirming that you have permission to edit the photo and agree to it being sent to OpenAI for that edit. For photos of other people, including group photos, you are responsible for having their permission. We apply usage limits and a monthly spending ceiling, and failed edits never count against you. You can report any AI result from the AI screen.

4. What is saved on your device

If "Remember my draft" is on, a reduced copy of your photo and your saved looks are kept in the app's or browser's local storage. Delete them under My looks & drafts, by deleting the app, or by clearing site data.

5. Exports and location data

Photos and videos you save are re-rendered on your device, so hidden metadata from the original (GPS location, camera details) is not included.

6. Permissions

Camera: only when you tap "Take a selfie". Photos: only to open the photo you pick and to save your results. No microphone, contacts, location or notification permissions are requested.

7. Subscriptions and promo codes

Face Couture Pro, when available, is sold through the App Store or Google Play and managed in your store account. Free trials convert to a paid subscription unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Promo codes redeemed in the app are tied to your install ID.

8. Analytics and advertising

No ads, no advertising cookies, no cross-app tracking. We count basic actions to understand which looks people love. Analytics never receive photos or face data. Ad personalisation is turned off.

9. Your rights and deletion

In the app: Settings → Delete my data removes everything linked to your install ID on our server. Online: facecouture.app/delete-my-data.html. Email requests: hello@facecouture.app. Residents of the EU/UK, California and other regions may have additional rights (access, correction, portability, objection); we honour them for everyone.

10. Children

Face Couture is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Security

All connections use HTTPS. AI provider keys live only on our server. Our server never writes photos to logs.

12. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the service changes and note the date above. Questions: hello@facecouture.app. Always Luxe, United States.
