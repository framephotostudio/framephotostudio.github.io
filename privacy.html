Face Couture for iPhone

The short version. Everything in Face Couture happens on your iPhone. Your photos never leave your phone, there are no accounts, and the app sends no data to us or to anyone else.

Your photos

Photos you open are edited only on your iPhone. Face Couture does not upload, store or share them. Photos you save go to your own Photos library.

Face data

To place makeup and to frame each size on your face, Face Couture finds facial landmarks (eyes, lips, brows, face outline) using open-source models that are built into the app. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Landmarks are not stored, are not used to identify anyone, and never leave your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server connection. It works in Airplane Mode.

What is saved on your iPhone

If you keep a draft or a saved look, it stays in the app's storage on your iPhone. Settings → Delete my data clears it, and deleting the app removes everything.

Exports and location data

Photos and videos you save are re-rendered on your iPhone, so hidden details from the original (GPS location, camera details) are not included.

Permissions

Camera: only when you choose to take a photo. Photos: only to open the photo you pick and to save your results. No microphone, contacts, location or notification permissions are requested.

Children

Face Couture is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds anything that sends data, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent in the app. Questions: hello@facecouture.app. Always Luxe, United States.

Face data

Face Couture detects facial landmarks (eyes, lips, brows, face outline) to position makeup. This happens entirely on your device using open-source models that are downloaded from Google Cloud Storage and jsDelivr the first time you use them. Landmarks are not biometric templates, are not stored, are not used to identify anyone and never leave your device. We do not sell or share face data, and we do not use it to train AI.

AI features and consent

AI features (Couture Glow, Couture Looks and the other AI tools) are optional and off until you choose one. Before the first AI edit you must tick a consent box confirming that you have permission to edit the photo and agree to it being sent to our AI provider (Google) for that edit. Couture Looks work on photos of one person; after the AI adds the makeup, your own eyes from the original photo are put back on your device so they stay yours. For photos of other people, including group photos, you are responsible for having their permission. We apply usage limits and a monthly spending ceiling, and failed edits never count against you. You can report any AI result from the AI screen.

What is saved on your device

If "Remember my draft" is on, a reduced copy of your photo and your saved looks are kept in your browser's local storage. Delete them under My looks & drafts or by clearing site data.

Exports and location data

Photos and videos you save are re-rendered on your device, so hidden metadata from the original (GPS location, camera details) is not included.

Permissions

Camera: only when you choose to take a photo. No microphone, contacts, location or notification permissions are requested.

Purchases

Face Couture has no purchases. If we ever add any, we will update this policy first.

Analytics and advertising

No ads, no advertising cookies, no cross-app tracking. We count basic actions to understand which looks people love. Analytics never receive photos or face data. Ad personalisation is turned off.

Your rights and deletion

To delete the AI history linked to your browser: facecouture.app/delete-my-data.html. Email requests: hello@facecouture.app. Residents of the EU/UK, California and other regions may have additional rights (access, correction, portability, objection); we honour them for everyone.

Children

Face Couture is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. AI provider keys live only on our server. Our server never writes photos to logs.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the service changes and note the date above. Questions: hello@facecouture.app. Always Luxe, United States.
